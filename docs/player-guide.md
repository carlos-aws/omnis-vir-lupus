# Field guide

You begin in the Hollow: a fire, a tent, and somebody who expects you to
come back. **Continue the story** starts your next operation. The world
screen shows all sixteen chapters and your current objective.

## Your origin

| Origin | Starting companion | Strength | Personal road |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Red | Mara, field medic | Stronger ability healing | The buried wards, escape, and the Carving |
| Gold | Cassian, duelist | More focus and a higher critical chance | House privilege, complicity, and refusal |
| Obsidian | Yrsa, oathkeeper | More health | The polar clans and the machinery behind their gods |

The early paths meet in Chapter 4. At the end of Chapter 3, a Red becomes
a Carved Gold while retaining the original origin ability and memories.
All four companions become available after Chapter 4; Ione specializes
in disrupting shields. Choose the support that suits your next encounter.

## Body and spirit

| Ritual | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Share a meal | Spend one ration; restore nourishment, resolve, and some health |
| Take a rest | Fully restore health, focus, and rest |
| Train your blade | Earn a little XP and resolve; three sessions per completed operation or patrol |
| Make a connection | Restore connection and resolve; build your current companion’s bond |
| Supply cache | Gather two rations while the basket has fewer than eight |
| Wash and mend | Restore resolve and connection |

Average needs of at least 75 give **+8% attack**. Low readiness slightly
reduces attack. Needs decay gently while away, with a bounded catch-up
window and a floor; absence never causes permanent harm or earns XP.
Camp is free to visit between encounters. An expedition’s checkpoint stays
where you left it.

## A turn worth thinking about

1. **Choose a target.** Its health, shield, elemental weaknesses, and next
   intention are visible.
2. **Break its guard.** Weakness hits remove shield points. Sunder removes
   a point on each of its two hits even without an elemental weakness.
3. **Use the opening.** Broken opponents cannot act and take increased
   damage through your next turn. Breaking also cancels a charge.
4. **Read the danger.** A charging opponent prepares a stronger hit.
   Guard cuts direct damage by 70% and restores some focus and health.
5. **Build Burst.** At three charges, enable Burst to empower your next
   ability. A regular Strike restores seven focus.
6. **Call on someone.** Your companion’s support uses your turn and has
   a cooldown. Bond improves it. Mara heals and cleanses; the others
   attack or break shields.

Burn and bleed cause damage over time. Weaken reduces attacks.
Regeneration restores health. Supplies use a turn. Withdrawal always
returns you safely to recovery, without a victory reward. Defeat preserves
the operation checkpoint and never deletes your character.

The battle log records recent actions. Animation speed can be changed
between 1× and 2×. Reduced motion removes walking, particle bursts, and
camera shake while keeping the readable controls and outcomes.

## A lifetime in eight phases

| Phase | Minimum level | Age |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ember | 1 | 16 |
| Awakened | 8 | 18 |
| Aspirant | 18 | 21 |
| Blade | 28 | 25 |
| Vanguard | 40 | 30 |
| Commander | 52 | 36 |
| Praetor | 65 | 44 |
| Living legend | 76 | 55 |

Growth follows experience rather than real-world waiting. You receive
XP from victories, completed operations, patrols, and limited training.
Level-ups restore health and focus. Main-story traversal typically ends
around level 78; endgame patrols can take you to the cap of 80.

## Equipment and decisions

Equip a weapon, armor, and a relic. They change stats and appearance.
Equipment has minimum levels; the quartermaster offers some stock a few
levels ahead so you can prepare. Forge owned equipment up to +5 for
+15% of its base item stats per upgrade. Later upgrades need sunstone.
Keep healing items and focus tonics before a difficult sortie.

Choices are recorded in the journal as mercy, defiance, or ambition.
These describe your history; **the explicit final choice determines the
ending**. Leaving and replaying an operation does not duplicate its
claimed rewards or rewrite an earlier choice. Patrols are repeatable.

## Controls and saves

Tap or click all controls. At camp, tap the ground or use **WASD / arrows**
to walk; **E** interacts nearby. All rituals also have ordinary buttons.
**H/M/C/I/Q/J** open camp/world/character/inventory/shop/journal.
In battle, **1–5** select the main commands and **B** toggles Burst.
**Tab, Enter, Space, and Escape** work with ordinary controls and dialogs.

Settings offers audio, larger text, reduced motion, difficulty, export,
import, and the previous checkpoint. Difficulty changes affect future
encounters. Keep exported backups: browser storage can be cleared, and
different devices, browsers, and site addresses have separate progress.
